Who this portal is for

This portal is provided to Allied Metals' contract clients. Each client's login is created by the Admin once a contract is in place, so there is no public sign-up.

What you can do here

Submit inquiries and quotation requests with blueprints and photos, follow every order through fabrication and delivery, and message the staff member assigned to you.

How we route your request

Each request goes to the matching department, then HR assigns it to a specific staff member who handles it and keeps the status up to date.
